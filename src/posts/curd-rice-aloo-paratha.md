I couldn't date a mallu chick till date, all my relationship's were interstate and I had to converse in a language which I don't know how to romance with. I always wanted to romance someone in my native language where I could crack some classic Vishnu jokes.

Life of an interstate lover is extremely rough and tough. There was a time when I had to eat curd rice for lunch and aloo paratha for dinner on the same day. Somedays it's hard to calm a Tamil Bhramin girl from bargaining in commercial street stores while somedays it's hard to convince a Mumbai girl that Eminem is better than Guru Randhawa. Somedays even though it's the right time to crack a Shaaaji Pappan joke, I have to settle with Vadi Velu or Johnny Lever jokes.

 

Somedays I feel that I am no longer a mallu and then my roomie calls and says **_"evideya nayintemone, vaa vellamadikkam?"_** (Hey, Son Of A Bitch! Let's Drink?)
