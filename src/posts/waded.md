I waded in the stream yesterday, gazing at the moonlit sky and wondering if changing my thoughts could help me feel less chaotic. I thought back to the sleepless nights I'd spent wandering through the old orchid garden, hallucinating on a random pill and hoping someone from the past would call..  
.  
Everything here is broken. The anarchy has left only a few weeds and orchids to thrive. The rest have faded over time, just like us. Every nook and cranny of this place was once filled with romance, but now it radiates revelations and redemption.  
  
Once, there were answers to every dread and anxiety here. There were people like us who couldn't run away, but had to find a place where anarchy couldn't touch us. This was ecstasy.  
  
Even as I write this, I'm dreaming. I'm not here emotionally. I'm looking for someone else's desires, and I don't want to be questioned. I've always been like this. I don't want to listen to your noise, but I want to understand your vulnerabilities. I want to see if you and I are clinging to the same redeemer.  
  
The mind can only fix momentary things. Time will eventually outdo it, and these phantasms may not repeat. Then I'll have to confront my thoughts again and convince them that there are no more desires left to move on.  
  
How impeccable were those days, reading a poem under that old oak tree and then staring out at the vast green grasslands, contemplating the thought that had just struck my mind?
