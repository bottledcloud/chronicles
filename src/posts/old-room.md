If someone asks me what do I miss in my life then the answer would be this room & it's memories.If I have ever invited you to this room then I would like you really much or you are one of my closest friend.

Imagine being drunk AF for 230+ days out of 365 days in a year ie reducing 52 weekends minus another 30 days of overlapping planned leaves. You might be wondering it's not real but I have lived through this routine for two straight years with no regrets and coming home after work with the anticipation that my brothers are ready to drink.

What makes this room special? It's the vibe & the predefined playlists. I have skyped till 4 in the morning with my ex gf and my friends would still wait for me. I would have just slept after sobering up but my roommate comes back after his evening shift and say **_"Maan! let's do this?"_**. I would die for such days now where I just had to say "_**Let's do this?**_ and my friends would order dominos choco lava cake at 3 in the morning".

I never let my friends leave sober who would come for a short stay over without experiencing on of the craziest day in their life. We drink to make memories is what my pack believed & still believes in, we even have our own dance moves & our own handshakes.

I have had one of the most favorite memory in this room & one of the saddest one too, I have never cherished solitude here but an abundance of care. We have fought over women & words but wine always brought us together. There are actions that would sparkle happiness rather than words like a chest pump with your fist means some surprise is there.

Imagine, 4 well paid IT professionals shared this house but we never moved out because we're just too lazy to find a new apartment. Before myself moving in, this room used to be their store-room and they just made necessary arrangements so that Vishnu could also fit in for the madness, imagine the level of friendship we shared.

One day let's roll back in time & do the same old shit we used to do before, I would love to spend with just two of you assholes [@the.high.wolf](https://www.instagram.com/the.high.wolf/) & [@jinishjohn01](https://www.instagram.com/jinishjohn01/) where we had only vapour and oblivion to be blamed & dance to coldplay's 'A Sky Full Of Stars".
