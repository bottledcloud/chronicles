I consider myself among the broken ones, the unloved, the one who carries the penance of his own masquerade while everyone sleep in peace. I can’t stay for more than two years in a relationship and that’s one of the key thing I insist when love starts to blossom. I was willing to bequeath relationships in order to find something new in me that the person before her failed to unlock.

Love is like that butterfly you see on your backyard, once she finds the most beautiful flower she steals the nectar and would never come back on the same flower ever again. The idea of Love is to find _**the right person in you**_ to settle with rather than finding _**the right person to settle with**_.

Few years back there were no captions under [#StopItVishnu](https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/stopitvishnu/) and none of the hands that fondled me could figure out that I could write until she came. Once she left I was left with thoughts of solitude and hatred that continued for months until it was overpowered by another women’s warmth. With her I learned honesty that I failed to grow my own where in my priorities was put aside for her smile until I could no more feel special. I left that paradox of honesty into the wind as I walked away with someone else who introduced me into poetry and within her there was a mélange of emotions that was ready to be infused into me. With her I learned poetry and slowly changed myself into a character for her to play with while I enjoyed the outcome of her imaginations until I was asked to walk into the woods with someone I recently met. I left the world of poetry for someone new whom I rambled into the woods without thinking about the impending outcomes. She was the one who taught me to listen to my heart even though it’s hard to say NO and the first one who brought me my first backpack and told me to travel without the fear of coming back and she was the first one to tell _**“don’t come back for me again Vishnu”**_.

I have experienced Love in different forms, some fixed the cure for my stubbornness but some lifted my chin & kissed to say don’t cry. If I look back I can see that I tried to run before I could walk but it also takes courage to walk away when everything around is happy because of you. I still might not have understood Love but to the long roads ahead filled with solitude, poetry & woods I am not scared to control the outcome of my life because I know that I'm the master of my faith not you nor any of your Gods.
