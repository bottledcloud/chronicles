Once I chained her to a memory of mine, a memory that had the backdrop of a piano fusion playing endlessly. I sat on my chair and watched her dance as she made turns in the air while the scarf played on her hips and I sit there imagining "How would I feel without her even for a day".

She was the epicentre for all the poetry I wrote, she was the beginning of the pain and twisted memories I had to endure and she was the end of the dark and empty paths I travelled. She was an afterglow I chased, the lover I am now, the music I listen to, the rose I held on a pale moonlit night, the reason I love night than day. Everything was for her, she was a broken piece of puzzle that failed to fit the society we lived in. She loved me for the words I bequeathed for her on that piece of paper few years ago even though I never informed about the lies I compiled for her.

One day, I left a note compiling all the lies I told her and left the place only with a photograph in hand. I thought I would find a better place without her, I wanted to start everthing over for myself and to chase a dream on a drag where I dont intend to hold any more hands but a stick when I am old.

I barely slept that night, I used to admire the dark and the woods but something was wrong somewhere and an inceassant sadness started to overpower my confidence. So I ran, ran into the wilderness like a madman, I have fallen many times by then and broke my knees yet I limped back all the way. It started to hurt on every inch of my body as the paths were made of thorns and stones but it wasn't even close to the pain thinking about the promise I broke. I lost my left eye on the last fall and I could barely see through the dark now. I knew I'm very close to her home now and I could see the lights fluttering on the wooden steps.

I wanted to apologize and ask for mercy on her feet, so I ran to her room and cried out her name but only to see her hanging down on the ceiling holding the letter I wrote for her few years ago while the piano fusion played endlessly.
