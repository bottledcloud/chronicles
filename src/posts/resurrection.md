My grandma became very cute and skinny on her 80’s with pure white hair and wrinkles covered most part of her skin. Her name was **Ganga**, sweet and beautiful as I remember or may be the most beautiful women I met other than my mom and she harboured a smile, she smiled just like a young kid caught stealing something from the attic.

Her leg had to be cut off because of high sugar levels and my poor Ganga was blind by then while her leg was removed.I still remember the day when she was gained conciousness and she was trying to feel her leg only to realize that its been removed. She was sad when she got to know that she no more had a leg even though it was inevitable for her survival. She started crying like a little kid who lost something valuable she kept inside in her treasure chest.

Once I was not allowed to leave for my Boy Scouts Program while she was admitted in hospital due to serious illness. That was the first time my dad called and informed me that my grandma might pass away in few days and the doctors can't do anything about it. 

I became really sad and I went next to her and said “Don’t worry you are just fine. You will live for another 5 years, so chill old lady”. Guess what, she passed that ordeal & stayed with me for another 5  more years.

I used to get food for her which my mom prepares and she would be sitting in her bed scratching her hair trying to kill the laces she could catch hold of while keeping her ear next to the fingers just to hear it pop. As soon as she hears my call that face would lift and ask _**“Is it Vichu?”**_ and I would gladly acknowledge it with a yes and spread the plates in front of her. I take her hand & touch each plates and tell her **_“this is rice, this is beans, this is curd, this is water”_** and slowly she starts to eat with no teeth in her mouth. Sometime she would cry while eating which was hard for me to stay and watch, I wasnt sure if it was because of happiness or sadness nor I will ever know.

She was suffering enough day by day as days passed while nothing could be done. Some days, when I go near her, she would ask me to come close to her so that she could kiss me and that was the sweetest thing I ever felt. I was her favorite child since the day I was born, the one who took care of me more than my mom or dad ever did.

She passed away one day morning since it was an impending scenario for everyone most of my uncle and aunts handled the situation but my mom was torn apart. I being one of the eldest kid had to perform the rituals but I was never sad about the fact that she left me forever.

I still remember that it rained really well that day and I was standing wearing a dhoti witnessing her being covered with woods until I couldn’t see her anymore. It was really cold standing in the rain witnessing the ceremony from a distance while the younger siblings of mine have no idea what to do.

My eldest uncle had to light the fire as the last ritual and everyone started to talk about the following rituals to be done once this is over but by then Vishnu couldn’t handle it, I broke down and cried. I couldn’t handle that pain by then, I didn't just lose someone I loved so much instead I lost someone who loved me more than I ever could. I cried all the way back home, that was one of the few times I lost myself somewhere which I couldn’t still explain. I still could feel the shiver when I think about that feeling.

**#StopItVishnu**
