**_What have I done to myself? I thought in time my mind would stop preaching me about things that I am trying to forget and even though I try breaking it down it to a granular level that thought someway manages to find a way back in and it pull me down from the center of my consciousness and it hurts me more than anything I have endured. I haven’t really dealt with depression nor have understood how it feels but what if this is it? Is this why I can’t pursue happiness._**

**_My playlists have been have moved from progression to sad melodies and I can’t pretty much make anyone understand what I am going through and I don’t intend to. I thought as I grew up the things inside my mind would fade and polish it with happiness, but I realized that I was always controlled by my mind just like injecting a dream drug whenever I realize that I am no more oblivious to my thoughts._**

**_I always believed that I have endured enough pain in my life so that I won’t contemplate over silly feelings but to make my point clear I had to drown in what I have once endured and if I look closely I am losing myself to what I have always been running away from._**

**__** **_How diabolical is life? I used to enjoy that essence of unknown happiness while being sad or gloomy but now I can’t nurture an emotion when I am sad, and the pillars of every thoughts have slowly eroded, taking me through a path I don’t want to be part of. Maybe this is what “V. Woolf” felt too, only to drown in a river with pockets filled with rocks so that no more thoughts can control her sanity._**

**_There was a time when everything felt real and the things that I have seen and felt were connected to a glorious dream but now I see no more. I only see withered trees, broken souls with bipolar thoughts, yet I find comfort in that because one day I will be over looked the same way saying he was different. I won’t need stones to drown because once I fall I don’t dream of waking up again and I would drift in that sweet abyss of continued silence._**
