Today I met a young girl who was trying to sell a bunch of red roses, she walked past everyone and asked if they needed one. I was standing next to her drinking a pop soda and listening to "Rising Of The Tide" by Blackfield. No one was interested in buying a flower from her, I guess the days of showing love with flowers has been ascended to dinner at a pricy restraunt or laying in bed going dutch on a cigarette.

She asked me  "_**Anna do you want some flowers**_", generally these street kids who sell items tend to walk behind you until you get mad at them but she wasn't even trying. I informed her that I dont have anyone currenty so that I can gift these flowers to and I said I would pay for two flowers but I cant take it from you. She asked me give it to the girl whom I have a crush on. I said _**"**_**thats a clever way(psycological move) of selling  but sadly I dont have the guts to tell that girl I even have a crush on her and I'm done with all these silly feelings now**_**"**._

She said the guy she loves sells the same flowers near Commercial Street only and every morning they would meet, count and tie these flowers together and that's the only time she could see him because post evening he goes and stays with his friends.

I enquired about her age and she said shes 12 with a smile and its was a like a tube light in a dark room, her face was covered in much dirt. I told her that **"When I was 12, I was on top of a mango tree near my home trying to pluck mangoes for my friends and I barely thought about love. You already have a lot of inhibitions to enjoy a happy life yet you are already in love with a random guy who you meet every morning."**

She _**ordered**_ me to ask her how does she know that he loves her? and she said, While we tied roses everyday, I found one rose extra and hes asks me to keep it. First I thought it was a mistake but everyday the same incident happened as he handed over me an extra rose until I realised he loved me and since then I had an extra rose to keep for myself. I kept that rose with me even though I could sell it off and make money. So if he has the guts to do that then you too can brother.

While I smirked, she asked will you do it brother? I gladly said, one day I would and I will meet you that day and buy that whole bunch from you for her. She refused to take the money from me & walked away.
