I should have written more letters to you,  
Not tossed like paper planes through skies of blue.  
Some turned back, retraced their flight,  
Whispers of thoughts that flee the night.  
Some I crushed in desire’s flame,  
Fog-bound roads without a name.  
I should’ve kept them, safe from harm,  
Like secrets folded in a charm.  
Not torn like dewdrops at the dawn,  
But tucked away, not simply gone.  
They came from silence, numb and deep,  
Where restless hearts forgot to sleep.

Even now, they call my name,  
Flickers of an untold flame.  
They paint my thoughts, though time has flown—  
Proof that we are never alone.  
Perhaps those words weren’t meant to send,  
But just to feel, to break, to bend.  
They linger still, like ghostly tunes,  
That haunt the mind in quiet rooms.  
When I think of you, I sometimes find  
A trembling thread inside my mind—  
Like poems learned in younger days,  
Now blurred by time in tender haze.  
I don’t find time the way I did,  
Too many thoughts I’ve kept well hid.  
Some are fragile, torn and tossed,  
Others stand though I feel lost.

While letting go of drifting things,  
My mind still clings to what it brings.  
I tied your memory with a thread,  
Invisible, but never dead.  
It tugs at me in silent space,  
In every room, in every place.  
Where déjà vu and dreams arise,  
Like mirrors dressed in new disguise.  
Some visions sweet, some bittersweet,  
Like echoes where past and present meet.  
And maybe they return to show—  
The heart remembers what we let go.
