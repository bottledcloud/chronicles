The road seems unchanged, yet the scenery whispers forgotten stories. I wonder, do these roads evoke such potent memories for you? If I could recall the whispers, the silent conversations with myself, weaving through these lanes, capturing them on paper would be a breeze. But alas, such isn't the nature of life; our deepest thoughts, fleeting wisps, evaporate just as quickly as they form. And in those fleeting musings, I strive to see myself through the eyes of those on my mind, empathizing with every ephemeral flicker.

Life is sometimes simple as an elbow pain where we tend to find the pain point by picking every nook and cranny of the elbow and accidently finding and missing the point at the same time. Just like that life changes that thought and its nearly impossible to trace back to find the origin of that granular epicenter. Like the roads I traversed today, one lane shimmering with nostalgic light, the other shrouded in the mist of "what if."

Sometimes, the simplest path to happiness is to surrender to a song. Let it become your friend, your companion for days, weeks, even months. Dive into its depths, let it wash over you. Forget the snowball fights you never had, the nights spent stargazing missed, the overthinking spirals too familiar. Leave behind the pressures of finding joy in every corner, chasing fleeting dreams, forcing warmth into a chilled heart. For in the grand scheme, life's canvas is ultimately empty for all. So find solace in the music, in the memories, in the quiet whispers of the road less traveled.
