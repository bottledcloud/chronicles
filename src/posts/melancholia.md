In life, pain the only real thing that is close to reality and rest all other emotions are passersby. I cant explain my mind right now, its happy, broken and lost at the same time and sometimes, I am oblivious to the purpose of my life. I have never been in this place before and this is very baffling for me because there are many things that needs my attention and I am not good at figuring out what's the priority of it. If only life was like Jira where someone can assign the priority of the thoughts so that I can tend to it rather than rambling around fixing this partially. If someone comes and asks me an example of what's bothering me, I cant pin point it because I feel my problems are shapeless.  
  
I can ease down all of these thoughts with a smoke but does it help me once I am back to reality? No! so there is no running away from it because I have never stayed away from an ordeal because I thrive on it. I can handle pain in any magnitude because that's the most realistic feeling because being hurt opens thoughts to new perspectives but happiness/joy makes me stay at the same place for a prolonged time without realizing that its a moments mess.  
  
Memories from the past doesn't reflect any of these feeling because past was a busy life with women and wine, there was only biased grief when either a bottle is empty or one walks out of the room but there will always be someone who walks in before that wound is mended. Life was tied between ecstasy and gluttony where emotions were not allowed to enter without permission and that busy life was fucking adrenalin rush every day.  
  
I not longer dip my toes in sand, no longer wait to enjoy a perfect sunset because I am comfortable in walking away and I let people around me contemplate on the view and I peek through them what I am missing. Even if I plant a million versions of myself at this same moment half of them would walk away because these are paper moments and how many of sunset moments can you hoard in your memory even though you have seen a thousand sunsets, how many do you think you can keep a track on because sunsets are like that its a momentary visual, appreciate it and move on, don't linger thoughts around it and fantasize it.
