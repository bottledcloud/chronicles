Adrift in this boundless ocean of existence, I float aimlessly, uncertain of any destination that lies ahead. The last vestiges of my memories have been drained, leaving me motionless, akin to the tides themselves. I hope that this version of me brings you solace, for in truth, you are consumed by melancholy and confusion. I could impart wisdom from this realm, yet it would be futile, as it is you who have set me on this journey. I have lingered for so long that the concepts of space and time have become alien to me. Here, eternity is the currency of existence, and night transforms fragments of reality into space, and vice versa. While beauty prevails, what purpose is there in solitary smiles? All other versions of us yearn for our creator, yet you remain fixated on the past, neglecting the connection we crave. Rest assured, your memories remain undisturbed, and the gateway to your mind's sanctuary is firmly secured from within. However, remember, your versions are drifting further away, some worth saving, even if they were once mere jetsams in your mind.
