I never read much novels only a bit of poetry and a torrent of Steven Wilson songs I harbored inside my veins. When I started to write I had no clue on what topics I should write about nor had any clue on how to pen down words and even if I penned down I didn't knew how to make you understand or connect it to a memory of yours. I wrote many pages about many contents proof read again and again until I could make you imagine it.

Every one of us can write and put words into a tranquil and inject to any kind of audience. If they think you are not strong in the words you choose or your grammar is wrong, fuck ‘em just keep writing. You don’t need to be on drugs nor the background of a melancholia to write because one of the most beautiful captions I ever wrote was during one of those train journeys to my native.

For basic you just have to find something that really hurts you or drags that rage inside you to an extreme that you are no longer the person you are now and if possible try breaking the walls of your comfort zone. Pull down memories from the paths you left behind which left a scar so deep that you judge the very reality of everything you come across. Run your mind through the darkest paths alone and be scared about the things you would encounter and definitely never share your feelings to anyone.

Find that tune inside your mind that keeps you sane and never hesitate to destroy things that inhibit you from pushing forward. I would love to read what you can write, let it be as simple as a sentence or a paragraph but put your thoughts into it. First you fail and then ignominy of being forgotten crawls in but don’t fucking stop.

If you still can’t find a reason to write then love someone, then you will find words that could destroy you forever. Never try to get inspired from anyone it never helped anyone nor it ever will you have never been in their shoes and vice versa and most importantly don’t let someone else remind you about the pain you have gone through other than you.

Once you start writing you will realize that you are no longer the person that you were yesterday. If you can't still find inspiration start listening to Steven Wilson songs, it will fuck your brain in every way possible.
