Yesterday I was thinking about Spiti Valley trip, which was quite memorable not because of the view but about the people whom I met on the road or the person whom I interacted. From the restaurant waiter who doesn't want a tip but want a drag from the charas ,to the waiter who brags about everything he is doing for us for a tip, to the person who said we won't make it back home due to our car choice, to the weird person who shouted at the tent that he is going for a solo trip leaving his girlfriend in the room 102 where she will be staying alone for a week.  
  
The whole trip was filled with uncertainty from the starting, Where to go? Where are we now? Are you sure we crossed Spiti? Where do we stay? Is there a gas pump nearby?. The whole trip had only two songs on repeat Cloudy Now by [@blackfieldmusic](https://www.instagram.com/blackfieldmusic/) & Chekele by [@sanjeevtmusic](https://www.instagram.com/sanjeevtmusic/) & I slept like a baby watching the glorious stupas & mountains passed through my window pane.  
  
I remember getting down at Tabo hoping to get a stay near a monastery until we entered there & the price was quite reasonable but even if it wasn't we had no choice. I randomly went to the backside of the monastery and voila! chilled beer parlor & we convinced to bring it to our room. Buddha tripping next to us, Cloudy Now on the phone, Star Studded Sky above what else you need to ask for. The world around us was tripping but a little tired too but that's okay, we got high with Buddha.  
  
The magnificent part of Lahul Spiti Valley unlike Ladakh, Uttarakhand or Sikkim is the vast emptiness of no human spotting. Like you pass through miles without spotting anyone to even check for directions. We were confident about going in the wrong direction on multiple occasions but it never really bothered us because we couldn't do anything because there is no help coming.  
  
One of the best place we ever stopped would be Reckong Peo,where we found a homestay close to the Kinnaur Kailash Range. Oh boy! the view was swashbuckling, you could literally stand on top of the terrace and jerk off but your jerk would fly away with the clouds to a valley somewhere far and fall on someone trekking illegally. Just Imagine!!
