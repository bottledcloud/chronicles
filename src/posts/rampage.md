I have been dreaming about missing you in reality and it's obvious that I do, but in my dream I could tell you everything I desired. I have been stuck with you at the same place over and over again, failing to find a shore to swim towards. You are always sitting next to me while the boat could barely hold us and there are no winds or waves that could move the boat. I think it's meant to be like this where you and I spend an eternity in dreams hallucinating over unspoken feelings and emotions. May be this constant space in my dream is harbored to keep your thoughts safe from reality where I can't show my deformity. I can rest my head on your lap and look at the skies at the night and recollect our memories. At night, I could feel your heart beating as the quietness is beyond imagination and I could wrap any desire I harboured inside me. May be I wasn't hallucinating because you were always there or may be it's you who threw the oars away so that you could be with me stuck in a space of constant loop. It's the same old black shirt and blue jeans and a sky filled with billions of stars rampaging against each other. How crazy is this space I created just because I miss you?
