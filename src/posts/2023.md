2023: A Lazy Man's Retrospective (with a Hint of Wisdom)

Sure, 2023 wasn't a total dumpster fire if I squint hard enough. But let's be real, for a laid-back dude like me, most years blur into a haze of "fuck it." Social media travel FOMO? Nah, that's for folks with free time and disposable income. My hefty EMI acts as a built-in firewall against wanderlust, unless my wife takes the reins and I get to play happy tagalong. Birthday celebrations? Same story. My inner circle is tighter than an Instagram "close friends" list, so December birthdays often fly by unnoticed.

Memories of 2023 are a bit blurry unless I jog my phone's gallery. But hey, I did conquer an off-road safari in Gavi with the fam. That was one wild ride, a real pain in the ass at times, but totally worth the thrill. And as usual I wasn't awarded the good husband plaque because I wasn't good at saying sorry. I'm more of a "convince-her-I'm-the-victim" kind of guy.

![](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/img_20240101_162547_792.webp?w=960)

My music mojo has been MIA for two years now. Blame it on the epic laziness that's become my default setting. And hey, Spotify, you can suck it! Healthy eating and workouts? Count me out, but I do religiously pay gym fees every year. Like clockwork. My pre-workout ritual usually involves a Herculean quest for my shoes amidst the shoe-pocalypse in the entryway. Why torture myself inside when I can get the blood pumping right there?

Speaking of Karma? That word's lost its rizz. Everyone's throwing it around like confetti these days. It's like atheism and devotion had a confused lovechild. I'm rooting for Gen Z to shake things up and ditch some of these outdated, often fascist beliefs. We millennials are already grappling with enough baggage, thanks.

This year, my soul belonged to Carl Sagan and his "Pale Blue Dot" podcast. For six glorious months, I drifted off to sleep with his voice weaving tales of humanity, planets, cosmic wonders, and the vast unknown beyond. That man saved me from the sleep-deprived fate of my late-shift friends. He's my new go-to guru for seeing the world through a different lens. Now, prepare to be dazzled with my newfound knowledge of the cosmos, black holes, interstellar messages, Phoenix A, the Event Horizon Telescope, and all the rest. Bow down before the wisdom of Smart Ass Vishnu! (Just kidding, or am I?)

So yeah, 2023 wasn't perfect, but it wasn't half bad either. It was a year of quiet wins, unexpected adventures, and late-night cosmic explorations with Carl Sagan. Not too shabby for a lazy guy, right?
