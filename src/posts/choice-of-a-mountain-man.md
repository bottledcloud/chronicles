I always wanted someone who would chase the madness of the mountains and run alongside me. I don’t want it to be named as love, love is predictable and I know where that hunger would lead to. To travel without a plan but with a map and stare at the unadulterated wilderness of the raw nature. A person who would value time more than money and know the fact that happiness is only real when shared. I came across many temporary faces over the years but no one had the audacity to break out of the comfort zone which always hurted me. There is a story of a lone farmer who lived near the river bank for decades but never climbed the mountain and when the flood came he climbed the mountain only to realize that there was a whole village on the other side. I want that someone to be a little selfish because happiness never drove humans beyond the comfort zone.

I always wanted someone who would come and eat with me at any place and not comparing the place with the standard of their living. Not to the fancy ones, not with one who’s fussy about the decoration or how splendid it would taste. One of the tastiest food I ever had been prepared inside an Indian Clay Pot supported by three bricks where my mother would blow air to burn the wooden sticks with a bit of kerosene oil and a match box. She would pour some hot curry on my palm and ask me to taste it and you can’t buy that feeling anywhere else because those where my roots. I want someone who should understand that the costliest dish isn’t always the best one.

I always wanted someone who could answer my questions with a “Yes or No” instead of saying “let me see if”. I always try to stick to that because it is a huge mistake to lead someone if you aren’t sure of the answer. Have you ever tried saying “I Love You” to your friend rather than to the person you have feelings for, it takes a lot of courage to do that but your comfort is somewhere else.

Once I was attending a live concert in Bangalore and the artist paused for a while and said _“**Turn to your right and say I Love You to the stranger next to you”**_ and there stood a girl wearing a Zeppelin t-shirt and pierced nose. Rest is history.
