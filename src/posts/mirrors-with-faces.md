I get very curious about everything I see and I prefer to enjoy that curiousness alone. There was a church near my home back in Alappuzha,Kerala where they play ambient prayer songs which I used to listen for decades. I used to stare across the window pane through the trees that cloaked the church, the songs would bring mild winds of sleep to my bedroom. I could sleep peacefully without zeroing on any specific memories.

Since then I had this urge to listen to a lot minimal music recordings especially by Max Richter, Antonio Vivaldi, Philip Glass, Ludovico Einaudi etc. Their recording were a compilations of raw voices for example a women would be reciting a poem while a type writer noise runs in the backdrop, compilation of the waves beating towards sea shores, a mix of piano and violin with discreet hang volume in the background, a hum of a young girl plucking the roses from her garden. It’s very hard to explain about it because it’s something that have to be felt to be understood.

I write when I miss someone, most times I intend to write something for her but it ends up becoming something else. Mostly, I write only things I have experienced or witnessed happening to someone I know. It’s easy to think but to put the right words to make someone else imagine the same needs pure passion.  I wanted to write about those memories or about those faces that have made a difference in my life but instead of respecting the time I have invested in knowing them they try to let off the burden they are carrying for a long while on me.

While trying to find my curiousness elsewhere I forgot about her, I failed to send her letters or flowers but most importantly I failed to tell her how much i have changed. One day before leaving her side forever, she held my hand tightly for one last time and asked **“Do you know the connection between you and poetry? I love both when I am sad.”** I wasn't sure whether to leave or stay.
