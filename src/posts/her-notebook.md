Recently I volunteered to talk to a women who was mentally ill and I was pre informed that she tried to end her life once by hanging and once by ripping off her veins but was miraculously saved both times. Post that scenario she became real quiet and spend most times reading books and poetry and Vishnu became very curious to meet her, to understand  and observer how fickle minded she was.

I met her & said “_**You look lovely today but I heard you f\*\*\*\*d up really bad last time**_”. She smiled and said **“_Yes, Vishnu I did_”**. I politely said **_"Please dont ruin my day with any mental break downs, I am really not prepared for it."_**

I asked her _**“Do you repent on what you did? I heard that you stood in a pool of your own blood while holding on to that pain”.**_ She said _**“I want to say yes but deep inside I know it’s not true”**_. I said _**“Let’s talk about your fears not the ones you are scared of but the ones that’s inside your head that wakes you up every night”**_.

> _**But before starting off a proper conversation she wanted to know how old I was turning this year.**_

 

She said _**“Vishnu, I don’t fear anything physically but I fear time and with age I became emotionally weak. I was scared of losing things I treasured, scared to share happiness with a random guy who would walk through that door, scared to stop all the imaginations I own and start something new, scared that my freedom will soon be a paradox, I am scared due to the inability to choose what’s right for me, whether to forget him or stand with him. The hardest part is what if I am making the wrong choice & I could never come back?”.**_

She looked at me and asked _**“Vishnu, Do you think I am too emotional now?”**_ I just nodded. She asked again _**“ So you just nod after I say everything I felt? ”**_ and I nod again. I realized that the hallucinations I had from that mush in Kodai was nothing compared to the ones she was talking about, she never slept for a week even on high dose painkillers and sleeping tablets. I always believed that suppressor medicines could overpower any mental thoughts but it wasn't. I have seen drawing done by people who had mental breakdowns but from her I realized that it was far worse. She said some days she couldn't close her eyes because someone would whisper on her ears to jump off the building. _**(and that dialogue really scared the shit out of me)**_

We talked for few hours and in between her mom brought a glass of lemonade and God! it was awful but I smiled at her and said thanks. She loved quotes and poems of Thoreau and Kahlil Gibran but I hate **Kahlil Gibran** for his frequent use of divinity in his poems and monotonous thoughts that he had been using in all of his works. He is one of the poets I genuinely hate. 

So I introduced her to John Clare and Walt Whitman, I recited “Bivouac On A Mountain Side by Walt Whitman” _(if you dont know then you should)_ & asked her to close her eyes & imagine what I recite. There was a long silence for few seconds until she cried and said _**"The stars in the sky Vishnu, the stars are very beautiful".**_

I could have given a solution for her illness but deep inside I know she wasn't ready to accept anything and I was more than sure that she wouldn't try anything stupid too because there is nothing that a little poetry can't cure. John Clare saved himself So can Joanna.
