Even though I only spend a year in Chennai, II have so much deep affection to that place. I miss weekend kabbadi games in Kovalam beach to the busy streets of Kelambakkam. At night I could watch the long stretch of ECR road from the terrace while vehicle lights gleamed making it one of the best incomprehensible moments in my life. Back then my college relationship was at its peak & so does the pages of my diary. Even though I hated being in a long distance love, I liked the world around me, it was filled with people whom I have never interacted before and it was intriguing. From having a crush on Krithika to doing physiotherapy sessions at Chettinad hospital, 1 year had almost come to an end. Local shops would have the best chicken noodles than any good restaurants esp. the one in Kelambakkam opposite to the hospital. From eating masala bun to enjoying films at Mayajaal, life was in perfect harmony. Everything was a piece of cake, the day was planned before it even began. I had some disagreement with curd rice so in turn curd rice became the only dish I never tried after being there for a year and till date too. Back then for getting decent printed T-shirts I had to go all the way to Spencers Plaza where Tantra was the only brand that had designs & they were the OG of printed T-shirts back then. I still remember sleeping off in the local bus until I realised that I am off track & if I had slept for few more hours I would have reached Pondicherry. The greatest betrayal was accompanying your crush to Kalyana Sundar temple until you realize that the temple is famous for solving issues leading to delay in marriage & she was holding a bouquet of flowers for her pre family determined fiancée. Chennai was the only place I had to go on a date with my long distance ex with her mother alongside & that too in Rajadhani Hotel where they serve hardcore vegetarian dishes while holding her hand under the table. It's been 13 years since I left the place but the memories are very vivid & I think that's the only place where I have left a version of myself without closure because that's the only version of myself who found peace and joy
