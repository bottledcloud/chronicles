Sometimes I pause and gaze at the consistent change that’s happening around me, few things are working as exactly as how society wired it to bloom and few are fiddling with that monotonous logic to run away into a paradise promised. Amidst these, I see few who are unfazed by these changes because their heart is at one place and their thought is somewhere else and these are the ones who learned how to exist without penance or contradictions. You and I belong here but we are separated by a torrent of unspoken words and distance.  
  
I always think how you make it to the night; I haven’t tried to decode why your memories play a mockery with the rest of my memories suppressing everything else before stumbling upon you and its hard to fathom how everything falls right back to where you and I took the first picture together. I always wanted my thoughts to have its own freedom but being in love with you and walking beside while keeping that inside was the hardest routine. May be hardest part now is not able to know your routine, not able to figure out how your smile would look, not able to be part of your chaos and most elementally not able to convey how much I still love you.Why hasn’t this feeling accepted a change after all these years, ideally every thought should but why are your thoughts so adamant? Why is your importance so vivid even with such a distance? I haven’t had a feeling like this before like waiting for an impending hug.  
  
Even at distance we think about each other, you read books and I read poems, we connect with silence and random words, if this isn’t love then there is no explanation to our life. My desire for you might not come to an end in this life and I don’t intent to teach myself how to bloom away from such a feeling because I have too much to lose if I stop thinking about you.  
  
So, amidst the chaos of changes around us I intend to keep loving you forever and I know you will too because if there was ever a choice, we would have found a way to stop thinking about each other. May be in time we would meet, may be years from now we would still tell each other I miss you and I love you.
