I hate when someone switches channel every other minute like something is about to happen in the other channels. My conscious would be expecting me to laugh at the upcoming joke and suddenly the channel changes. I can't handle that shit in my life, so I stopped watching television when the remote is with someone else.  
.  
I can't sit with someone who credit God for whatever they gained in their life. I used to get irritated when my Amma say "Thank God, You passed the exam. I was praying to God all day". I spend countless hours studying at late night without jerking off during Kerala monsoons where power cut happens every now and then. All those hardship I took to graduate and I let God takes the credit? Nah!!. See I am not against you believing in an unknown entity but don't rub it on others.  
.  
I don't like people who share motivational quotes or videos on social media because it's an easy way to figure out that you are very vulnerable. It's fine if it's fitness motivation, I get it because it's hard to get a sex pack if you are eating rice for lunch and dinner. So motivational videos for gym is athukkum mele exceptional.  
.  
I have a rule, If you have a desire you should present it but don't be like those pesky mallu guys in the Middle East who keep DM'ing girls in India just because their loneliness can't be shown off like the car they own. Either work on the approach part or learn to understand the clue and move on like a good person. I know you guys bring lot of pistachios but nuts don't work always, get the pun?  
.  
I don't like FRIENDS series and I haven't even watched a single episode of it. This is a classic example on how easily Vishnu can hate something without even experiencing it. Nah, it's because I am committed to Two & Half Men. I promised I won't see any other comedy series that would overwrite Charlie's dialogues. TAHM is my Jam, I have even memorized few dialogues just in case the situation demands it.  
.
