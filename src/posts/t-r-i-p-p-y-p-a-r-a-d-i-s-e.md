On the way to Sivasamudram falls this landscape had caught my attention multiple times but I could never stop or enjoy the pansy the nature was offering. Most importantly the biker club I rode alongside believed only in reaching destinations rather than finding curiousness in the paths we cross and in simple words they were pussies. So I gave up the membership of that club and started riding solo.

So this was the year when I found an offline girlfriend which means I didn't slide into her DM asking for number via any Social Media app. I did what a real any real men would do i.e. I lied to her impeccably from day. Since mallus have a very bad reputation in Bangalore, finding love in it's purest form is always out of scope.

So, to celebrate my girlfriends(**_ex now_**) birthday I took her to the same place I was always curious about and she was pretty much okay since she was desperately looking to take a break from home. I have predefined stops if I have already been through that road before so I stopped near that special bun and tea shop run by an old lady near to the highway that I have been talking about since the day I have been to Sivasamudram falls.

![2016-01-17 01.09.32 1-01.jpeg](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/2016-01-17-01-09-32-1-01.jpeg)

I took her to the that place, a place far away from the madding crowd and untouched by urbanization. Tamarind trees covered the whole valley while local barley and corn plants stood high enough that the grasses were never to be seen.She was like a little kid trying to find a place to lay down and admire the pansy beneath her feet.

I like those days when she light a joint and vicariously explain the pleasure in living in a place like this unworried about the impending future. We shared drags laying on that field ticked by dew drops until we could no longer hold hands.It took a while to come out of the pin prick and I got up only to realize that it's been more than an hour.

![new1q3](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/new1q31.jpg)

It's close to evening and calfs were running here and there I told her that it's time to leave as we have to reach city before dark. She was reluctant to leave the place by then and asked to stay with her for few more minutes. I could see the kids from local village were already enjoying the evening playing their own games in and around these grassland.

I like her when she is silly as she wished to build a home near the center of the field with windows on all sides so that she could never lose sight of the field and also swing attached to the main tamarind tree so that she could read her favorite book from there.

I am not sure if they realize how beautiful their villages are or how unadulterated their thoughts were but there we both sit and stare at them hoping our life could be beautiful as this even for an hour.I haven't wished her birthday till now but I guess even if I did now it won't outshine the happiness she is feeling now or may be I night never know.

I broke up with her after few months but I have been to that place again with someone else. Love is beautiful, isn't it.?
