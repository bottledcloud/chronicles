I want to lie beside you on a meadow and stare at the vaulted skies striking off those stars that never amused you with your fingers. We don’t have to hold hands or be passionate instead we are ought to enjoy the silence of the night that the world is conspiring for us at that moment.

Her bosom was like a jade vine waiting to be blossomed into perfection and her lips like Juliet Rose which made every other girls existence in vain. I have been with her countless times but her smile never gets old and between that smile and passing time I lived with the fear of losing her everyday.

She starts to hum slowly and steadily. I started feeling restless that night as something was not right about the night.

_Have I felt this moment before staring into an abyss of celestial with her unfolding all my deepest secrets?_

> **Humming**

It's beautiful and painful at the same time harboring these thoughts that could steal her away from me. I wished if this silence could whisper on her ears what I am feeling right now the feeling of swimming across an endless ocean.

_What could I possibly hide from you? (coiling on the finger she pointed at the stars)_

![IMG\_20171001\_125851-01](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/img_20171001_125851-01.jpeg)

> **Humming**

To lie in a field alone taming all the monsters in my head was much easier but with you beside me closing my eyes for a second drags me to another dream. I have been here before of that I’m sure, the meadow next to the river and this cold breeze is reminding me of someone who called me every other day. In my mind I can remember a vague image trying to regain its shape but I cant figure out who exactly it is.

_What if it’s a dream reminding me of someone whom I knew before?_

> **Silence**

Why every hum you make is dragging me into a dream that is reminding me of someone else? Could you please stop the humming, there are too many version of myself in my dream now and I can’t find the real me.

Can you wake me up now so that we can go back home? But then you closed my eyes again and started to sing.

> **Continued Singing**

**My Love! Have we been to this place before?**
