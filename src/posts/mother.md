I used to lie to my mom about my exam results just to make her sad and out of anger she would scold me and start crying involuntary just because she raised her voice at me but I would tell her that I was just joking and I got good marks.

But this was not the case if I scored less marks , I would run to the terrace and stand close to the else so that I could scare her with the fact that I would jump if she came near me. The only way I am coming down to ground zero was only if she promises not to cane me. She would say "_**D****on’t worry Vichu, I won’t tell dad about anything**_" and the first thing she would do is feed me really good and wait till Dad arrives back from his work and submit me as a present to him so that he could cane me really nice.

Even though she did the same thing every time I loved her more and more because she would feed me dinner from her own hands at the end of the day. No matter how much hardship she had to face while growing up or while taking care of her old blind mother, she was the epitome of love. She cared for everyone around no matter they are related or not, she would sit and cry for someone else's sadness. I think part of that gene have passed down on me and I blame her for myself being very emotional sometimes.

I grew up watching her lighting the lamp at 6.30pm sharp and chanting slokas she knew or trying out a new one she learned recently. Sick or not, she would wake up early at 5am so that little Vichu could get his glass of tea in his bed and till now I can't remember a day when she was around and I woke up without having tea. She wont listen to me but I have to listen to her even now like I said I am still that 5-year-old boy for her even though I turned 29 few months back.

I sacrificed many things that were precious to me just to see that smile on her face. No matter how many things I bought for her she would say "_**why are you buying this old women all these fancy things**_" but the next day, while walking on the road some random aunt would come and say "_**Hey Vishnu,** **t****hat saree was really beautiful, how much did you pay for that**_". That was my mom, I didn't have to check-in facebook but whole locality would know that I bought her a saree and its good. 

There were many days of chaos and loneliness and every day I wished I could hug her and tell her “I miss you so much” and to sleep in her lap while she read a fancy weekly digest. Whenever I go home, She would hug me & say sleep a little more as tea is getting ready and when I wake up she is there with a cup of tea in an old silver glass , the same silver glass used since my childhood.

Sometimes I know that she is wrong but I just nod and say "_**yes mom you are right"**_. No matter how old I am, she still feeds me if she had the time and she would open her mouth along with me involuntary enacting whenever I swallowed a handful of rice.She knows I save money but she’s scared that I would travel somewhere one fine day to places she haven’t heard of.

> Whenever I felt sad, I would remember about those days when I sat on her hip and munched on a piece of fish fry while she points at a crow and say “Vishnu, If you don’t eat this, he will come and eat it, so eat”
