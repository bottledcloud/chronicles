I moved on from a lot of thoughts and I should thank this pandemic for giving a break from some routines. I had a lot of time in learning a few tricks and I must admit that I am becoming a little smart at coding now. I keep myself very occupied and I don't let myself be idle, I either check stock market or find patterns for the next intraday trading. If you don't understand the above sentence then you aren't saving your money in the right way, once you step into stock market its like the same feeling when you jerked off for the very first time, what the fuck is happening!. Lets not divert from the topic, we aren't going to discuss about stocks here. I just want to say that its kind of my interest now apart from 9-5 job.

Well, how long its been sober? I really don't know and only my body knows. I miss gym not because I workout and all, I like doing cardio while listening to my playlist. The gym instructor Babu likes me because I pay rent at the right time and I hope he dint go broke during this pandemic. I left my bike in Bangalore and here I have to ride my dad's Bajaj Discover which my dad thinks is the best bike of the decade and expect him, we all tell him this bike needs to be sold. I tried convincing him to buy a gearless scooter but he doesn't budge. My generation of parents are a little crazy and emotional at the same time.

I don't see any significance of Facebook in my life and I would probably deactivate it because I am unsure when was the last time I liked someone's status or profile pic. Instagram is overwhelming but its a good place to keep in touch than WhatsApp because end of the day everyone joins Instagram for no reason. I think its a good place to keep in touch with your old acquaintances and ex-girlfriends. Some of my ex-gfs might not ask how I am doing but would ask to give a follow to an Instagram page created by her sister, like regular Karma wasn't doing enough damage. Rest of em' are still in touch with occasional meme forwarding or generic conversation and I am glad that even after all these years we could still be friends plus Instagram is a good place to stalk and get away with.

Few of my friends who are couples told me that they prefer still staying or adjusting in Bangalore rather than spending time with their parents during this pandemic because they cant adjust staying at in-laws. I feel sympathy for them because if not now then when do they need your support. Since 2010, this is the first time I am staying with my parents for a long time and I realized that my parents are hiding some much physical pain that doesn't appear via phone calls and are suppressed via prescription pills. I will write about the experience at home in another blog because recently there was a scenario that moved me a lot. So, what I am trying to convey here is that while you are trying to adjust away from home you fail to understand that home needs your care more than ever.

Yesterday I was realized that ever since I moved to online music streaming, I never listen to the ones I have already on my phone memory card and its the same song which I have locally I try to stream. Its like the meme "in search for gold we lost a diamond", The songs in my phone brings more memories and add a random thumbnail art of a random picture from my gallery also, its like you are listen to Pink Floyd but the album art says "www.daddydilruba.com", its really awkward to play in front of someone so I switched to poweramp which downloads the correct album art. I have this fetish over listening to songs with the correct album art, may be that's the reason why I prefer Spotify. I consider those who listen to artists like "Vancouver Sleep clinic" ,"Angus and Julia Stone" or "Iron and Wine" etc as musically smart because not everyone stumbles on these bands, its either a luck or a suggestion by someone close. I still use the old winamp on my laptop to listen to music just because I like the skins of it and the amount of modifications you can do with it, always nostalgic.

I have been missing good Biryani like the ones in Bangalore, here in Alleppey restaurants are fucking bad, like the word fucking isn't serving enough justice. Its just one or two decent restaurants that's it. I missing having spicy Andra Biryani from Annapoorna mess with a coke and halfway realizing that your nose is sweating and ass is on fire. Its a hard task to walk back to desk after lunch but the taste is worth dying for. We recently came to know that the owner passed away due to heart attack but the people who are still running it are making sure that it still live up to the standards. Biryani can pretty much solve any fights.

There was a period where gaming was everything like PC games, the amount of time spend exploring maps and playing side quests was always fun but progressively it all went away. The amount of scolding I received from my gf's for not making time for skype were unreal but little they knew about pressure of entering a boss fights without using cheat codes, its a mental preparation plus only if it saves right before the fight, some fights you might need to start all over again. Only if you have played Devil May Cry or Metal Gear Solid you would understand how hard it is plus I always play hard mode because the ones below that mode are for noobs. During college playing Counter Strike was the best thing, enter into a random room and play until you get an alert from the data provider because 1Gb was the standard limit every month. You play a YouTube video and there goes you 100mb in 5mins, forget about accessing Pornhub the moment the whole page loads with the gifs and pictures half of your monthly quota would have been depleted. Life was a slow back then accompanied by a PC running with 256mb RAM and 40Gb memory. Does 40Gb HDD exist anymore? Naa!!

I hate getting throat pain, its a never ending relationship which I have to live with because of my preference to cold stuffs. I generally don't drink hot water, okay! I hate hot water. Hot water gargling is a horrible experience but have you done betadine gargling, oh boy! that is one bad experience. There was a time when a nasty throat pain hit me and it raised my body fever to such a level that I could barely move and then throat started getting infected and it reached a level where swallowing a bit of your own saliva was next to impossible. The air you breath through your mouth would hurt in such a way that breakup pain is like cake walk and the moment you take betadine gargle, you say praise the lord. So now I take cold stuff but I take it sip by sip like you sip whiskey on rocks. I wish none of you have to go through that ordeal because you have to stay sober for a week until you can consume anything cold so yeah! but hot water still sucks. Have you ever?
