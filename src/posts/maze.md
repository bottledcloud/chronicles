Everything is a loop, from waking up to falling asleep. I look around and it's all designed to repeat itself. From the first spiral of thought that moves gently through time finding another thought yet passively handling day to day work without making me conscious of the maze I am living inside. Like a fool sitting next to a cliff believing that a delusion of flight can be achieved in a nick of time by a sudden jump. A one eyed monster from space is staring at me, she understands that I am disturbed and I am close to finding the cause of this glitch in time and she doesn't like that so I should be careful about it. I have seen everything from that little hole in the tree, a space filled with unknown shapes and colors or am I supposed to stumble into it just like that?. I can hear people calling my name from far and close yet I can't figure out the direction and I think that monster is playing tricks at me. I can be only passively angry here because everyone else will notice my change in behavior so I scream only during a night filled with thunder. I can't figure out these colors yet nor the shapes but I am someway able to recognize the smell that space has, I have felt it throughout my life. Everything is a loop here apart from my dreams, they don't repeat and that the only place where she can't control me and through that space I will escape from this world. There was a ripple in the water which doesn't have a center yet they all believed someone threw a stone.
