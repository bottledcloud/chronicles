You shouldn’t have come into my life, everything I feel or listen someway reminds me that you have been missed deeply and I can’t put words to undermine how much vulnerable I am now. I was strong even though how painfully I spend days under that roof and I never had to think before my actions until you came.  
  
How could you anchor me to a shore while my heart was dragging me to a less ruined place? I enjoyed meadows alone, watched lovers catching taxis from my window pane but now I have lost my calmness, everything I see connects to your smile and hurts me because you aren’t near me now.  
  
I fashioned my life alone, tread alone through paths unknown with no fear of impending dangers but now I understand that the thought of missing you hurts more than the fear of getting lost.

- I had no boundaries because my heart could hold limitless pain and I had nothing but  uncertainty in front of me. Ever since I met you I would wake up in the middle of the night and ramble here and there in my room because I felt like you called.  
  
I never asked for love and banished everyone who stumbled on my path offering love yet you stayed back and walked alongside me even after I strangled you with a torrent of pain. Why didn’t you leave me even after all these torments? I was hurt for the first time when I turned back but couldn’t find you and since then I never tread alone.  
  
I came back to find you amidst the chaos of the busy street but couldn't find you and as time passed I experienced something which never happened before, a drop of tear rolled down my cheek and fell on the sand. I had no strength by then as I felt love for the first time and I felt vulnerable to even the calmest breeze that followed after. So this is love after all.
