My old man was simple, never gave any motivational speech or quotes for me to get inspired. I was never like him, his world revolved around whole family. I haven’t even seen him going out to meet his friends nor did any of his colleagues come home. I used to wonder how he could dedicate his whole life just for family and his cousins.

I have seen him staring at the broken walls and roofs, the ones that needs to be fixed by next monsoon. May be that’s the reason he never invited anyone home until he mends all these things. He owned a Bajaj Chetak whose age is almost identical to mine which still works. He would sell anything (even me) but not that scooter and he would never let me ride that scooter.

Rare are the days when my dad comes before his office hours to pick me up from school and the happiness I felt when I spot him standing near my school gate was unreal. I watched the whole world passed around me while holding tightly across his tummy leaning towards his back.

I have never told him (not even once may be) how much I love him nor wished on his birthday but all the gifts I bought for him might have spoken. He goes through my salary slip, file my taxes, and check my bank records but never asked for money till now. He would say if you have excess money just give it to your mom she will handle it for you.

He would brag in front of everyone that **_“My son is working as a consultant in an American company (stressing on American)”_** then some days he would shout in front of everyone **_“Listen, My son is an engineer working for an MN**C** who doesn’t even know how to fill a bank challan, how old are you now son”._**

> Whenever I got hurt while playing, my dad would say **_“This is nothing vichu, it will heal soon”. He held me close and said “When you grow up, you will realize that some things won’t heal no matter how much time it takes”_** and he was right.
