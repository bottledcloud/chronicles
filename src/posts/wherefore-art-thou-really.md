For a second lets assume that I don't think about your or in other words miss you okay? Your disappearance from social media wasn't a shock for me but I did presume that one day your profile might pop up somewhere and I feel content that yes you do exist amidst the chaos. I don't think its a necessity that you would think about me or my thought would pop out into your mind out of a sudden reaction. I probably think you married and left to Europe somewhere far from my reach, probably its good that you are not near either. The only way to get a slight idea about you is the LinkedIn profile you still update or had updated a while back and I am quiet happy that I can someway read that you are safe somewhere but I did fail keeping in touch or being not there to share your feelings when you cant share with anyone? I think I regret a lot of that because somewhere deep inside a seed of your longing is still there, it still installs into my thought whenever I see something that reminds me of you. Last week I saw that same tree where we used to drink tea and smoke cigarettes' while you said you are leaving me and I was a little unsure how to react to it because I felt like someone robbed me of something valuable but I should have expressed what I felt for you even before everything started to fall, may be I should have shared my feeling to you rather than writing on captions back then.  
  
I would ping you out of a random blue day when my thoughts about you cant be overpowered because there is no way to stack you right now or back then. But all you give me back is a psychological metaphors where in someway you make me realize that I am happy and you are still trying to figure out your life. All I could do is stalk your roomies stories or post but you never appeared on any of them and I realized that you are detached from theirs too. I am sorry that I couldn't be there but wherever we reached a common ground you always retraced saying that it wont work or it would cause more damage and I would like always, agree to what you say. I would then go back and ask myself , did I try enough to make an amendment or as usual go back listening to "Deform to Form a Star by Steven Wilson"?

I want you to understand that I never stopped trying to reach out to the people I know about you and if few of our mutual colleagues reached out to you is because I was desperate to know what you are doing? If you would have received random pings from ex colleagues then I would have someway convinced then to get some details of your current ordeal. I know if I reach out directly the information I receive would be reduced into half or may be there is no point in answering this delusional guy. Lets pretend I am delusional for a while but then I would have tried to create a havoc of pings but I am not, I am still waiting for that day to arrive when I would get a ping from you where we could start off the conversation from the place we stopped last time.

I never smoked a menthol cigarette since you left, next will always be with you because the start was with you. I can either wait until I am alive or may be next life I can recollect the memory if we are born into a universe where the tech is much savvy. Until then no more menthol cigarettes okay? I sometimes look at old pictures, may be oldest ethnic ones if you remember it correctly that was the first pic ever but little did I thought I would find comfort under you crazy smile and messages. Ever thought what happened was meant to happen at some point of time but somewhere deep inside I regret it.

I hope you find solace in your new place, may be you find comfort in the company around you and I hope they are making you giggle with new memes and puns. I don't know assume anything about you now because I don't have any information to assume on, its like staring into sunset its beautiful knowing and not knowing at the same time. I am hoping to get a ping from you someday, if not I can wait but for sure we will meet one day ,sit an speak for a while and share some cool tricks you learned or may be teach how do this social media detox on a serious note?

May be I would ping you again and wait for you metaphorical replies every year until you figure out that I am still the same old kid that shared that menthol with you while you made regular tea for me while everyone else drank ginger tea?
