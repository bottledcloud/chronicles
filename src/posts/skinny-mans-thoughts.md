> ### **FACTS I FIGURED**

- I used to be **5’2”** till my 10th but when I graduated my 12th I became **5’9”** so I had no other choice other than believe in miracles. (BTW those are measures of height: don’t get confused).

* * *

- I should have born somewhere between July & August. Well I can’t blame my old man on his 30’s because my creation would have been on the bottom of his to do list. So, I became a Capricorn and I have no idea what a Capricorn does or doesn’t.

* * *

- I hate listening to break up stories even though you share explicit content of you intimacy. You should know that love is always out there, it’s just you have to go & find it your own. A note to be taken care when you are finding love is “Think with your mind not with the thing hanging between your legs”.

* * *

- I don’t like papaya and I have wondered why someone would name it Papaya which rhymes to nothing. If you ever order papaya juice while I am next to you, they I would say  **_“enthu oomban aanu papparekka juice medikunne”_.**

- When my ex gave birth to her second child my dad someway found a picture of her from my old wallet and sarcastically said **“_avide onam randennam kazhinju, evide ethuvare mahabali vannitilla_”**. I had to show my stupid ass face to the whole family & point out **_“You guys knew I had a girlfriend?"_**

* * *

- I have this curiosity to eat things I find very curious and if you observe my friend circle most of them would be plus sized because I order food and If I won’t like it then it’s up to them finish it. That’s my secret of being skinny.

* * *

- I have a very good memory about everything I have experienced and every faces I have interacted with. So, If you are trying to flirt with me avoid using numbers for e.g.: **_“When is your dad’s birthday?”_**. I would have to breakup with you then and there.

* * *

- Once a girl told me _**“if you have told me this three years ago, I would have been yours”**._ So if any of you owns a special feeling for someone (unless it’s Point: 2 ending) be open about it and don’t carry it so forward that one day you will realize that first sentence would hurt you more than the kick on you tender balls by your best friend.

* * *

- If you feel very romantic about riding in the rain or smelling the raw earth after a good rain, please do it yourself. I know how a good fever and throat pain feels like so I will Netflix and chill but you can go ahead book an Uber and go to cubbon park.

* * *

- I have no idea about the features of bikes or cars, recently only I understood that a single puncture on a Tubeless Tyre would cost 100 INR. Imagine 10 or more punctures, then I would have to sell my kidney.

* * *

- In the gap of a year I have traveled to Delhi-Ladakh-Kashmir-Spiti Valley-Shimla-Manali-Chandigarh (approx 1 month altogether and excluding South India trip). I don’t own a DSLR and I have no idea how buttons works except the ones in my game controller. When I came back from those trip, I became so black that I could do stunt double for Akon in any of his pop songs or a black shadow for anyone.
