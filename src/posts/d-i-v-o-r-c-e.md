Few days ago my best friend got divorced, technically they were both my close friends. I was the one who convinced her to marry him after a year long evaluation of his pros and cons. I convinced their moms/dads for their relationship and in return I was the one who heard a lot of trash talk from both sides not to mention about a good grip on my neck by her elder brother.

So even after all these help I did for them he stands next to me smoking a cigarette and informing me casually about the divorce and she explicitly informed him that I am not supposed to call her regarding this incident.

I remember sitting with her asking her to find something unique or a common ground in him to start with and since she liked classic rock songs I made him  listen to Jim Morrison, Frank Zappa, Pink Floyd etcetera until it worked.

I am sad because I lost a lot of time that could have been used for myself. I remember my mom used to tell me _**“When you run to help others make sure you haven’t forgotten yourself on the way”.**_

I don’t understand this concept of Divorce these days. What happened to “being together forever”: the only reason why you married?. Dating for 5+years and still not able to understand each other is fucking ridiculous. What happened to the promises, the letters you wrote, the money you spend on gifts and mostly what happened to the memories you created together.

Why do you ask a third person about the decision you have to make in your life? You will be constantly torn between the choice of  the judgment you have made and the one who could have made only if you could listen to your heart. Why can’t everyone sit and talk it like old school rather than finding solution through inspirational quotes and YouTube videos?

I look at my Mom and Dad, they haven’t spoken to each other probably till their wedding day and are staying together for more than 30+years overcoming every ordeal from silly fights to monetary issues. The only problems they had to worry was created by me but end of the day my mom loves my dad more than anybody even though many times she would scold my dad when he says “age begets more laziness”.

Tell me? When you are old, instead of sitting next to her counting the freckles on her beautiful face, What are you going to do? Stare at a fucking wall?.
