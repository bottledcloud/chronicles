Around me I only see suffering, sometimes I hesitate to be happy because out there I know is someone is suffering. If you believe that travelling is a cure for everything then you are making yourself a fool because travels are ephemeral. We fail to catch the emotional suffering of others yet we always try to be empathetic because that's easy to say. You can see people handling emotional breakdown by travelling and I always felt it's going to amplify when you are back to ground zero especially when you are working a 9-5 job. Being alone with age and no intimacy comes a lot of delusion which fabricate this wayward thought. I have seen across many and most of them have no clue where they are driving their life towards and what gives them peace is this strong idea of travelling. I have been there, looked across snow cladded mountain, laid back watching the meadows until I realized this isn't how it should be. All I accepted was a little transient happiness just to cover up what's necessary and then I looked around and I saw everyone believing in the same curse. We all need to talk to the one that suffers inside, the one who really needs attention to. Like they say, Grass outside is always green but not necessarily a home for you to live. You don't need a vacation to be happy, you need someone to love and fight. Like I always say, travel to enjoy the place not to rinse off a pain temporarily.
