“Come, walk with me,” she said—a voice so familiar it felt etched in my marrow. “Let’s revisit your old thoughts, the ones scattered like forgotten relics under the attic’s dim light. Play your songs if you must; this will take a while,” she murmured.

“First, pick something immovable, something you abandoned while rooting yourself on new paths. Recall them. Acknowledge them. Tell them you’re back, stronger this time. Let them crawl under your skin, unraveling your composure like chaos erupting in an asylum. Allow the merit of strength to dilute into madness. Let these echoes of the past find rhythm, then wander boundlessly until they understand—it’s okay to vanish without a recall. Know that once you set them free, they’ll disappear for good. And when you return here, seeking the solace of loneliness, you’ll find nothing. They’ll all leave, one by one, if only given the chance—a trauma pill for every buried secret.”

She paused, her tone shifting. “What’s next, kid? When everything’s resolved, what remains? No arms to run back to, no unburned bridges to cross. That’s when the real loneliness begins—a dark room leading to another, endlessly searching for light, sleepwalking through a maze of absence. Was it ego that hurt you? Or was it the act of desiring itself that left everything in shadows, the residue of leaving etched into your every shade?”

She tilted her head. “Can you feel it now? The tone, the weight? Your feet itch to move, but your heart still lags, unsure, misplaced. Try holding my hand for just a moment. Walk with me. Maybe then you’ll understand the fragility of staying, the courage it takes to remain and find solace.”

Ahead, she gestured. “There lies a bed of eternal rest, lit softly by fireflies and brimming with fragmented dreams. But that’s not why you’re here, is it? Why haven’t you cried yet? Every lost sheep wails for its shepherd, but you—you walk unshaken, with a tune lodged in your head. The corpses of your desires lie still, untouched, and the solitude hasn’t yet pierced you. What are you looking for now?

“It’s time to stop searching the abyss. There’s nothing there. And yet, how can I wake you from this slumber? It’s me weathering in your loneliness, isn’t it? You’ve lured me into this limbo, this in-between where shadows play tricks. How can I leave now?”

I smiled, a faint trace of rebellion in my eyes. “Find a tune,” I whispered. “Let’s dance.”
