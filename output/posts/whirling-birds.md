In my dreams I am always stuck with you, watching birds whirling in the sky at twilight, smoking a dube but heavily panting like there is shortness of air around. I wish I could tell you my broken fantasies but our consciousness have already been ruined once and no words could describe how much I missed you in my dreams. I don't have the luxury of dreaming about us staring at the star studded skies because I become a firefly at night and I fly into the wilderness with all thoughts erased from my memory and wait for the darkness to consume my dying light.

Sometimes I dream about myself standing under that tree which blossomed a little red fruit while smoking a cigarette with you and that was the last dream where I could see your face. I remember it rained once and you want to go back but all I wanted was to watch you while the rain drops rolled down your cheek and die in the ground. Now I can no longer dream about your face nor the dream allows me to peep across your hair that plays in the wind but I could watch was your lips that speak of me while staring at the sky.

![](https://i2.wp.com/www.ndns.in/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/fireflies-e1532340454294.jpg?fit=1024%2C576&ssl=1)

The saddest part of a dream is that reality it designs for me when I think about you, there could be an infinite design for us but every time it's the same sky and the same you and an unforgivable tune of sitar being played from an unknown direction. I wish I could dream about you smiling and running into my arms to kiss me but I become a firefly by then and fly into the wilderness. May be it wasn't the birds whirling in the sky nor it was twilight, it would have been the fireflies that flew into the sky looking for the horizons. Can I be a firefly now?
