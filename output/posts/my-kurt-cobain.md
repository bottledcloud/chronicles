> **Time Period : 2002**

Every evening after six:thirty I used to visit our temple situated at very close proximity to my home and I spend few minutes of quality time there either alone or talk to the association of grannies who are older than the trees there. One main reason was why I reached at that particular time was because a pretty hairy girl used to come at that time. There is a also demerit of being there at that time i.e I have to make sure that I bang the temple bell till the Pundit finishes his routine chats around the deity with his mini bell.

Its easy for the pundit to use a tiny bell but Vishnu being 35 kg at that time had to ring a bell placed at 20 feet high for 60 seconds without missing a beat. (_**Apply Physics Bitch!!**_)

![giphy (1)](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/giphy-1.gif)

So while returning back I saw my bother yelling my name & asked me to come over to his place as his parents traveled outstation. I never had much interaction with him because he is very hostile and aggressive plus his friends were literally those primitive assholes I ever met. Even though we were of the same age he hit puberty and sex know-how way before that I did and but not brains.

![giphy (4)](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/giphy-4.gif)

He said come post eight in the night & asked not share our plans with anyone. I was a bit excited because I thought this was the bro code where he introduces me into his lifestyle and more importantly the porn world where he would be give few puberty-adolescence mixed speech about how to achieve​ self-control.

I reached his place a little early to avoid being caught and without wasting any time  he took me to his room where that single historic 256Mb RAM DDR1 PC was kept which supported only CD Reading. I never had this much excitement in my life ever, my heart pounded like a goods train while he arranged two chairs facing the screen.

![giphy (6).gif](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/giphy-6.gif?w=1122)

From the ashes of the dust behind the book shelf he bequeathed a CD, tapping on his chest to show that your bro have got this covered. The excitement was unreal as I was allowed to insert the CD into the drive, hoping to enjoy what’s going to unravel in the next few seconds of my life and I was ready.

![giphy (7).gif](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/giphy-7.gif?w=1098)


Camera runs behind a man who’s singing a Rock song and out of nowhere the singer pulls his dick out showing the entire crowd how crazy he was and his name was "Kurt Cobain". That day could be recalled as the only day I felt happy seeing someone else dick because he was singing Lithium and he really didn't care about anyone. Plus another incident where he just came to stage because he was ill and he said few words to his fans and fainted. _**(couldnt find his dick.gif)**_

![giphy (8)](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/giphy-8.gif)

Cobain voice had the rage of a wildfire, you can’t imagine or sing his song without imitating him. I have literally fed on “Where did you sleep last night” since the unplugged version has been released and I know most of you guys have done the same. Since then I have had seen countless videos of his concerts, YouTube unplugged sessions or interview with MTV or any article about him I could get hands on. 

![images](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/images.jpg)

Vishnu became your first class fan since then but only to realize that you passed away few years ago . I would have send letters to you on your birthday of yours or probably buy you Lead belly’s guitar if I am rich by then. You would have been 50 by now and the world would have been a better place too if you kept singing. You and Dave could have done tours together reminiscing the good old Nirvana days so that I wouldn't miss you this much as I do everyday.
