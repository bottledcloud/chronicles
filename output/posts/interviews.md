 I miss good old interview vibes where I go and attend interviews without any preparation just to get an idea of how the expectations are set. There were few interesting turn of events occurred during my interview days.  
.  
I still remember getting ready for an interview in the morning and as I was about to ride off to the location listening to ACDC I got a call from the HR and before letting her speak I said I am running a bit late but I am on the way but She took a pause and said the guy who was supposed to take interview for you have passed away yesterday night and until we find a replacement we are keeping this job on hold. Ever since I told my friends this news they would tease me saying that he might have read your resume yesterday night.  
.  
I once went to Deloitte to give a second F2F round but the interview vibe was on another level because they gave a burger and a Pepsi at first and asked me to get comfortable but little they knew about my preparation. On top of that they were giving cream biscuits not even regular biscuits, I understood that either they are going to fuck me really good inside or they are just really nice people. I became a little comfortable after seeing an old mate munching on those burgers inside pointing fingers at me 'Re Maga, come ra. It's chill only eat some biscuits'. I got rejected in the fourth round but if the HR would have told me there were 6 rounds I wouldn't even have applied. You cant just spent a whole day eating breakfast, lunch and dinner while giving interviews in between.  
.  
HCL was fun, I had promised that I would appear for an interview if the location is Electronic City Phase 2 and further but this was fucking Jigani and it feels like going for a bike ride to Mysore. I kept on riding until I forgot that there is an interview scheduled and I reached some kind of rural area and they told me there was a small waterfall close to 10km. I reached in formals to see that the waterfall, took some selfies and came back. I blame the songs I was listening to and also the distance because I have a very tender heart.  
.
