I am at drift always, unbalanced with the desires and thoughts I can't weed out. Sometimes I am broken, yet I enjoy looking at the prettiest stars and listen to the radio loud even when she walked out the door. The rain has now settled yet I can see some drizzle dripping down the window pane and some of them have soaked my cigarettes too but it doesn't change the fact that sky is beautiful and its moving sideways with the direction of the wind. I wonder if those lovers holding hands across the flea market are just like me when they come back home, may be one of them might be broken like me or may be they are spirited one who enjoys the sunsets cuddling on their balcony. Some of these birds whirling in the sky looks familiar, they come and rub their beak on the same window pane for no reason or do they scent my emotional drift? . The room has seen a lot of faces and who walked out of the door is more like an enigma now. I look around to see there are memories spread across every nook and cranny and I tread carefully not to stumble on any. Lord has no place in this room as it was never been blessed from the start instead a poem from Rosetti was glued on the wall which serves the same purpose.I can see a version of myself in that crowd staring right back at me without moving an inch while everyone walks past around him like a glitch in time and I can see a bird on his shoulder hopping from one shoulder to another like he is grounded to a nail. I presume I haven't connected with few rouge versions of myself who failed to agree with the thoughts inside my head. I think we all need some internal conflicts to be delivered into afterlife. Afterlife would be peaceful I presume. I would be an eagle there, floating in a sky of vastness, soaring up in the sky at night looking for those prettiest stars where my mortal form overlooked at those for happiness. May be I could reach there and disappear into the abyss but that only gives freedom to one version of myself while other millions are scattered around in different timelines looking for answers.
