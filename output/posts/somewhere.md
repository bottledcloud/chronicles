Is music really necessary to write or think about you? You are somewhere out there, afar from my reach now and my constant brood about you might not reach your thoughts but its still munching a part of my peace. I saw a similar tree few days back that gifts those little red fruits, I could still picture you standing next to that and smiling at me before everything fell apart. I had pictured us catching taxis like lovers from 80's giggling over left over coffee mark on the lips, smirking before popping the mint inside cigarettes and sliding into the next day with a cuddle. Your thoughts would dissolve when I was with you and I felt calm and empty inside at the same time, like staring at a sunset that about to disappear into the horizon.  
.  
I recently read somewhere that when we look up at the stars, its millions of light years away and we are looking down at the universe not the other way around and the universe we know keeps on expanding boundlessly into an abyss of infinity. Yet, I look up to the stars thinking one day you would do the same, realizing that we are looking down into our past for answers and we failed. Amidst this chaos called life we find peace without realizing its just another delusion that repels the desire to being loved.  
.  
Lets take a step back, lets roll the dice once again when we are together, when there is a dimension where our thoughts can find a common ground even for an infinitesimal pause. I like to pursue that strand because somewhere there is a version of you who never left and somewhere there is a version of myself who asked you not to leave.
