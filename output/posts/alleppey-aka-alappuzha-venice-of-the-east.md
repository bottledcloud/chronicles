If you ask me what to do in Alleppey, Kerala, I might give you the usual tourist suggestions. But if I were in your shoes, I'd follow my own nostalgic itinerary. To truly enjoy Alleppey's charm and beauty, start and end your day at Alleppey Beach.

Begin your day early with the sunrise. Walk along the seashore from the lighthouse side to the park side. In the early morning, you'll see birds playing along the shore, their little ones flipping across the sand, retreating quickly when humans approach. The morning waves seem darker, as if they carry the secrets of countless dawns. In the distance, you’ll spot fishing boats heading out, much like they have for generations, not to return for a day or two.

From the beach, take a walk towards Dutch Square, passing through Masjids, Gujarati Street, and Churches along the way. Each step is a journey through time, exploring the museum near the Power House Bridge, and then heading to the morning market. Arrive early to witness the market's bustling beginnings, as lorries from various states unload vegetables and fruits onto the streets, with workers hustling to store them in godowns. The fish market next to the Church starts bustling, with people rushing to find the freshest catch.

Return to the beach via the parallel road, leading you directly to the lighthouse through Jail Road. On the way, you'll pass by the FCI godown road, where tons of wheat are transported to storage. This area later transforms into a photoshoot or stunt spot where kids perform bike stunts, reminiscent of youthful days spent seeking thrills.

Head back to the beach and walk left towards the railway station road, where local fishermen sell fresh fish at affordable prices. You can get a bag full for a bargain.

On the next day, head to town but avoid the crowded city center. Instead, visit Premier Bakery for some delicious chicken puffs and rosemilk, bringing back memories of simpler pleasures. From there, make your way to Punnamada and continue until you reach a toddy parlor on your left. This place, next to the canal, is perfect for a relaxing day off. Order a variety of dishes, like tapioca and beef curry, fried anchovies, choora curry, or crab mixed with fish eggs. Pair your meal with cold water or a sprite to balance the spiciness.

On your way back, stop at a shop on your right for a boost sarbeth and then head to Mullakkal Temple. Sit under the banyan tree and watch Balakrishnan, the city’s famous elephant, enjoying his meal.

In the evening, return to the beach after 6:30 PM when the crowds start to thin out. Recently, shacks have opened up where you can enjoy a good tea while munching on roasted groundnuts, just like the evenings spent with friends. Make sure to ask for sliced onions with your roasted groundnuts for the full experience.

As the sky turns crimson, head towards Cafe Catamaran. The walk to the café, especially if you have a loved one with you, can be one of the most beautiful moments ever. It's a short 500-meter stroll, but you'll see kids playing in the sand, beach cricket in full swing, and couples and friends sharing tea and moments.

Once at the café, sit on the sofa, order a drink, and gaze at the beach while live music plays. If you're alone, this might not be as enjoyable, but if you have company, the night will leave you with great memories. You'll find yourself wondering whether to return to your room or stay and watch the moonlight illuminate the beach, creating an almost surreal experience, much like the unforgettable nights of the past.

Alleppey, with all its charm, leaves you with a heart full of memories and a sense of peace that stays long after you’ve left its shores.
