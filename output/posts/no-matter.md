No matter where I am at the moment, my mind would remind me that this is not where I belong, this is just a drift, like a version of myself looking back in time. In the north I can see boats as tiny as my fingers floating at the horizon. Surreal isn't it? my mind began rambling and my thoughts already started to wander, ultimately leaving a body staring at that moment oblivious to the reality around me. As long as I can remember, I liked being lost and I can recall many instances where I found peace being lost elsewhere. I do understand the world around me in a better perspective than anybody else and may be that's the root cause of everything. The world itself is a catalyst, unknowingly reacting to my thoughts making every silly moments melancholic like the sunsets, it doesn't hurt anyone right but why doesn't it work the same way with me?. It's almost twilight now, the beaked birds are doing their final drop into the lake aiming for their final catch before they head back to their nest. How easily the day ends for them? Do they even contemplate on anything before resting for the night?. Look at me now, staring at random moments happening around me, tying unrelated emotions into my thoughts so that my mind can filter the chaos from it and thrive on. Look at me now? ready to drift with me once more?
