I owe my uncle a lot, right from the day he started picking me up from pre school on the front seat of his bicycle to dropping me at the railway station in his autorickshaw whenever I returned back to Bangalore.Poor guy he is, who loves me a lot more than his kids and I grew up shifting my place from the front seat of his bicycle to the pillion seat of the same bicycle.

![giphy (9)](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/giphy-9.gif) I remember the day when he forgot to pick me up from school and it was slightly raining, almost every kids left the school but I stood there crying asking the caretaker "where is my uncle" and she replied that he hadn't come yet.I cried until he came and he held me close and said "I am really sorry da, I slept off".I realized that he couldn't stand seeing me cry and he never ever came late since then.

![giphy (10)](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/giphy-10.gif)

He drives an autorickshaw with a label named Ganga (named after my grandmother) and that's what his livelihood is/was and no other source of income he had. He is a person who stores an abundance of love inside his heart which I have seen and felt ever since I saw him and half of my childhood was on his shoulders than on my parents.

![Screenshot 2018-12-02 at 01.14.05](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/screenshot-2018-12-02-at-01-14-05-e1543693515549.png)

He is the one who took me to watch theater films & also bought cassettes so that everyone can watch it together. He used to bring me daily a packet of groundnuts from the local seller who roams around my hood. He is my moms closest brother & if I give him some money to spend then the first thing he would do is to inform her that I gave him some money.

![giphy (12)](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/giphy-12.gif)

His name is Gopakumar but for me he is my Gomaman and it's been 30 years I have been using that name. I don't think I can ever been a brother who loves his sister as much as he do & I remember the day when he was admitted in hospital & my mom was crying relentlessly telling me he is not well.

I am proud to be born to a middle class family who ordealed through many crisis to become what I am now but when I look at him who drives an autorickshaw for living and still be the same person I have met 20 years back and still loves me the same way as If I am picked back from my school.

> Look at him, he doesn't even know how to use a phone properly but he is my childhood hero.

#StopItVishnu
