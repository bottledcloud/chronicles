In a world that exists only in dreams, where the night sky is adorned not with stars and moons, but with an endless array of floating lanterns, I find myself adrift. Here, water stretches as far as the eye can see, enveloping everything in its embrace. Houses float aimlessly, their lantern-lit windows casting soft glows onto the rippling surface below. Boats glide through the water, each carrying its own cargo of luminous lanterns, weaving through the watery labyrinth like phantoms in the night.  
  
Yet, despite the surreal beauty that surrounds me, I cannot shake the feeling of profound isolation. In this dreamworld, where desires and emotions are dulled by an otherworldly hum, I am lost. How did I come to be here? Was it the echo of a forgotten wish, or the subconscious yearning for solitude that led me to this ethereal realm?  
  
As I navigate the dreamwaters in my boat, memories flicker and dance upon the shimmering surface, like reflections in a hall of mirrors. I catch glimpses of faces and places long forgotten, their fleeting images whispering secrets of a life left behind. Each ripple in the water seems to carry echoes of past joys and sorrows, mingling with the gentle hum of the lantern-lit sky above.  
  
Approaching a towering mountain that rises from the depths of the dreamsea, I am drawn towards its peak, where the lanterns gather in a dazzling display of light and color. Here, atop the mountain, I find solace in the mesmerizing spectacle unfolding before me, like a grand symphony playing out against the backdrop of the night sky.  
  
But even as I bask in the beauty of this dream world, a lingering sense of unease tugs at the edges of my consciousness. How long will I remain trapped in this realm of dreams, adrift in a sea of lanterns and memories? Only time will tell, as I continue to wander, lost yet strangely at peace, in this enchanting dreamscape. #StopItVishnu #Dreams
