I never accepted change in my life and I stayed attached to people who inflicted a tiny change in myself. I get very emotional if I have to change a routine and I end up dwelling on the fact that an inevitable change is soon imminent.  
.  
In the recent past I understood that our vulnerabilities should never been shared no matter how staunch our relationship(s) are. Once I had a dream, I was laying on my mothers lap unsure about the time and place around me and I was talking to her about my thoughts and how I pretend to avoid it. I had the chance to share with her the vulnerabilities I harbored over time and repeatedly cried to convince her that I was no more the happy kid that she raised.  
.  
May be dreams are the only place were I could be brave and free at the same time. Lately, I invested a lot of time in understanding myself, I wanted to know what I really like and whether I am judging things based on its emotional or mental value. I don't welcome anything that would hinder my mental peace so I reduced my circle and now everything is at bay. My WhatsApp doesn't make much noise nor does Instagram because I realized that I have gained the maximum from those and there is nothing that could get my attention.  
.  
My friends circle didn't grew but my playlist did abundantly. There was answer to everything inside it , a perfect place to hide everything.  
.  
.
