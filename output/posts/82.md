I boarded my very first flight in 2012 to Delhi in order to break-up with my girlfriend(ex). I just disappeared from Bangalore one fine day & reached back three days later without informing anyone.

I was scared to visit Delhi as my uncle & aunt stays in Mayur Vihar & I didn't want to stumble upon them one fine day morning in Rajiv Chowk Metro. So I stayed with my friend Yogesh in Pari Chowk, Greater Noida where the chances of anyone recognising me is less than zero.

Technically there was no need to travel that far for any breakup but I like to visit historical places So I intentionally created a cause for it. We broke up on the first day itself at Humanyuns Tomb which used to be a good place but currently allocated to teens who can't afford Oyo Rooms & same goes with Purana Quila.

I had two more days to spend there, I broke up with her again the 2nd day at Lotus Temple & broke up with her again on the 3rd day at Palika Bazaar. She even bought me a pair of shorts from Palika Bazaar & if I remember it's an air conditioned market.

![](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/screenshot_20180223-165830-01.jpeg)

So the next day I had to catch the return flight to bangalore. She called & said **_"Hey, I will come & meet you before you leave"_** & as promised she came & bargained with an auto wala to drop me at Terminal 1D & left for her work in urge.I reached Terminal 1D just 20mins before the flight & was trying to weave of the impending sadness of my life.

Suddenly the auto guy told me **_"Sir, Idhar Indigo Flights Nahi Ayega, Ye galat terminal hain? Aapko T3 Jaana padega aur vo thoda door hain plus 300bucks extra padega"_**

I was literally fucked that day & I had only 220 bucks in my wallet & this guy wasn't ready to take me there without the required money.

I informed him that I am just returning back from a bad break up & I am really worried if I would miss my flight. He became really sad and took my bag inside his auto & said **_"bheto sir, I will take you there as soon as possible & pay me only 200"._**

We took almost 10mins to reach there & by then we became good friends and he even asked me to come & meet him at Pahar Ganj next time I visit Dilli.

The sad part was those shorts won't fit me when I came back to bangalore.

#StopItVishnu
