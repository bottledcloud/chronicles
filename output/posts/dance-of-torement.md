Agnus danced all her fears away, the voices inside her were too overwhelming for her control. I have seen her at the corner of her room, scared and paranoid to sleep due to the painful repetitions of the nightmares she once endured.

She would turn on the tape and slowly move around the room before picking up the momentum, it feels like she is following an afterglow until its stand still. She once told me that when everything is still dance starts. The lights were turned off, only lights through curtains were visible and she would dance around that space like a little butterfly catching up with the wind. She would stretch more than her height without tripping & that's my favourite part.

I would tap my feet gently to the music she played while she danced around the hall like a young bird who learned to fly who moves from one tree to another carelessly. I wanted this moment to echo in a beautiful repetition but for her it's the dance of her torment, the dance with the devil as she calls it. 

\[caption id="attachment\_143" align="aligncenter" width="661"\]![Capture](https://thestonedchimpschronicles.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/capture.jpg) **Kochi-Muziris Biennale**\[/caption\]

The tear marks on her cheeks mixed with the kohl are still visible but she never asked for sympathy or forgiveness but danced for salvation. Hard are those twists and turns like the cold breeze that chills your spine in winter. She never turned or looked back at me ever since she started her dance, my existence was merely felt there and it felt like she created a world of her own while she danced, it's was pure magic. She calls her dance a prayer, a dance to forget the torment induced by a group of men that night while they burned her house down.

I, an orphan neglected by everyone around me, who is scared to wander alone, who failed to follow my master had a revelation when I met her. I sat at the same place every day and watched her dance, at times I wanted her to take my hand and dance with me but never she asked nor showed any empathy towards my smile.

Today, I walked towards her and asked **“Will you teach me how to dance? but I don’t have anything to give in return”**. She smiled at me for the first time and asked me to hold her hip tight. I still remember who cried that day, such a beautiful repetition indeed.
