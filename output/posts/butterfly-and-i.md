_There was a butterfly in the woods who had a wing deformation, it could only fly upto the height of a grass, while every others enjoyed nectar from the flowers he fluttered on top of grass and dandelions to suck on the dew. He never understood the taste of sweetness yet the little one enjoyed what the ground could offer.  
  
Every morning he would flap his broken wings to see if he could reach up just to see how the flowers would look like, sometimes he would stand close to the wind to see if it could throw him up and land on top of one but there has not been a lucky day_

_The little one loved playing with the flightless friends on the ground and he would wake up early to enjoy the morning dew that falls on the grass but no matter how content he is with his small world everyone else would still call him a Loser._  

![](https://www.scitecheuropa.eu/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/butter-696x392.jpg)

  
_I have always been that butterfly who couldn't reach where other were, I chose to stay with things that made me content. I never ran to find an opportunity thinking that I would miss things on the way so I walked slow and stumbled on things I found curious because my heart demanded it. May be I never explained what my thoughts are to others, may be I never will.  
  
I have dreams of an eternal paradise where my thoughts doesn't decay, I have access to every memory and fantasies that I once harbored. If given a choice, I would choose to think about love on an endless loop as there was nothing else to be recalled as fun.  
  
I feel like I have lost my mind somewhere and I am not able to figure out where. I can only feel the pain yet there is no chaos to find the origin of this hurt. I am not dreaming well these days, may be there is a limit to where my mind can organize emotions. I tried to mend this pain by substituting karma but sometimes I feel I am burdening myself with pointless conversations.  
  
I wish I was a little butterfly so that I could be friends with you, we could enjoy the morning dew together and be content for what the present have given us. When they call us as losers it may hurt but little they knew about finding happiness in places where others failed to stop and explore.
