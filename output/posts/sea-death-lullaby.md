There were only three songs on the Gramophone that my Granddad gifted me before his death. First one was the recording of sea waves smashing a shore, second one was the recording of a women narrating a suicidal note while typing it on typewriter and the final one was the recording of a mom’s lullaby for her kid to go to sleep.

Last year the Gramophone stopped working but even now I could hear those songs transcending progressively while I enter the room. These songs piled up more sadness in my life than anyone else but some days these were my savior.

Have you ever stood near the sea-shore with your eyes closed waiting for the waves to swipe your feet? The sound of the sea waves were soothing at first but it held an abyss of relentless pain of someone trying to find something lost. I dream of drowning with her into that bottomless sea while holding her hands but before fading into the last breath she would kiss me and leave my hands. I could see her drowning into the deep but then I would wake up breathing heavily.

The suicidal note narration is one of the most intense ones as there was no pain in the words she spoke but boundless love for the one who left her in vain. Meticulously, I ascended to that person whom she was talking about and started to understand why she spoke of my missing while I raid misery from my own mistakes. Some nights I could see her at center of the room typing letters for me while staring right at me without uttering a word.

Mom’s lullaby takes me back to my childhood where I enjoy zero pain but love from all directions. I could see the smile on my face sleeping peacefully while she sings my favorite lullaby. A gentle rub on my forehead and a kiss would drag me into a sleep where I am hypnotized for a period of time until she wakes me up. I love this part of my dream where I’m not bound by anything, no nightmares and noises from the shore can hurt me anymore. I dream here until the record resets and starts from the beginning.
